# Educational purpose and project notice

## Purpose

Cockroach Chat is developed and published solely for education, research, and controlled
experimentation with Bluetooth LE networking, distributed systems, and encrypted messaging.
The source code, documentation, examples, and APKs are experimental learning materials.
They are not offered as an emergency communications service, a personal-safety tool, or a
system suitable for disaster response or other critical operations.

Conduct experiments lawfully, on devices and networks you are authorized to use, with consenting
participants. This project does not authorize interference, unauthorized access, surveillance,
or other unlawful activity. You remain responsible for your conduct and for identifying applicable
privacy, radio, cryptography, export, and other legal requirements. An educational purpose does
not create an exemption from those requirements.

## No assurance of safety, security, or availability

The implementation has not undergone an independent security audit. Tests, demonstrations,
screenshots, design documents, and the use of established cryptographic libraries are not
certification or guarantees of security, anonymity, privacy, delivery, availability, compatibility,
data preservation, or erasure. Messages may be lost, delayed, exposed, or retained by recipients.
Radio transmissions can reveal a device's presence. Device or operating-system compromise can
defeat the intended protections.

Do not rely on this experiment for emergencies, personal safety, or essential communications.
Read the [threat model](docs/threat-model.md) and [security policy](SECURITY.md) for technical
assumptions and known limitations. Historical planning and research documents describe scenarios
and design goals; they are not current deployment recommendations or promises.
